mountain

A mountain is a natural landform that rises prominently above the surrounding land. Mountains are generally higher and steeper than hills and are formed by movements of the Earth's crust, volcanic activity, or erosion

forest

A forest is a large area of land covered mainly with trees, shrubs, plants, and wildlife. Forests are one of the most important natural ecosystems on Earth and help maintain the balance of the environment.

sea

A sea is a large body of salt water that is smaller than an ocean and is often partly enclosed by land. Seas are connected to oceans and cover a significant portion of the Earth's surface.
